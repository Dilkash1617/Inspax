Inspax training programs combine practical industrial knowledge, code-based learning, and modern inspection technologies. Our training tracks are designed to help professionals develop the technical confidence and evidence-based skills required in demanding industrial environments.

01

Industrial Non-Destructive Testing (ASNT NDT Level I & II)

Rigorous qualification tracks designed to master subsurface flaw detection and surface discontinuity evaluation under strict international code frameworks.

  • Volumetric Methods Mastery: Advanced instruction in Ultrasonic Testing (UT) calibration, signal interpretation, and Radiographic Testing (RT) film interpretation.
  • Surface & Subsurface Diagnostics: Comprehensive hands-on protocols for Magnetic Particle Testing (MT), Liquid Penetrant Testing (PT), and high-precision Visual Testing (VT).
  • Code-Compliant Evaluation: Training focused on execution and defect grading in accordance with ASME Section V, AWS D1.1, and API standards.
02

Welding Inspection Technologies (AWS & CSWIP Prep)

Technical training focused on the physics of metallurgy, welding procedure validation, and structural weld defect identification.

  • WPS / PQR Analysis: Training personnel to accurately interpret Welding Procedure Specifications (WPS) and Procedure Qualification Records (PQR).
  • Weld Defect Morphology: Microscopic and visual identification of undercut, lack of fusion, porosity, and hydrogen-induced cracking (HIC).
  • Structural Compliance: Defect boundary evaluation mapped to structural codes to minimize costly on-site weld rejections and rework.
03

Protective Coatings & Corrosion Control (BGAS-CSWIP & NACE Alignment)

Specialized technical tracks designed to eliminate premature coating failures and manage aggressive environmental degradation on critical steel and concrete assets.

  • Substrate Preparation & Ambient Controls: Quantitative training on anchor profile measurement, surface cleanliness metrics, and psychrometric calculation windows.
  • Application Surveillance & DFT Metrics: Precision mastery of wet and dry film thickness verification, inter-coat adhesion testing, and curing log governance.
  • Advanced Holiday Detection: High-voltage and low-voltage spark testing methodologies to isolate microscopic voids and discontinuities in critical protective linings.
04

Next-Gen Inspection: AI Use Cases & Digital Asset Intelligence

Next-generation frameworks designed to integrate machine learning, computer vision, and structured data verification into traditional QA/QC field workflows.

  • Computer Vision for Automated Defect Detection: Training inspectors to deploy and calibrate AI models for real-time automated surface flaw, pitting, and crack classification.
  • Sensor Data Pipelines & RAG Integration: Building automated workflows where field NDT data feeds into centralized contract and code compliance engines for instant verification.
  • Digital Audit Trails: Developing step-by-step electronic logs for field readings to support data governance and commercial traceability.
05

Heavy Industry Equipment Inspection (API 510, 570, 653 Prep)

Elite preparatory tracks tailored for in-service inspectors managing critical static assets.

  • Pressure Vessel Inspection (API 510): Remaining life calculations, degradation mechanisms, and repair protocol validation.
  • Process Piping Inspection (API 570): Thickness measurement locations (TMLs), corrosion-under-insulation (CUI) tracking, and pressure testing safety.
  • Aboveground Storage Tanks (API 653): Tank bottom settlement evaluation, cathodic protection monitoring, and shell integrity mapping.
06

Technical QA/QC Governance & Project Turnover Auditing

Operational training engineered to bridge the gap between physical field testing and flawless commercial project delivery.

  • Quality Dossier Compilation: Systematically structuring manufacturing record books (MRBs), material test reports (MTRs), and NDT clearance logs.
  • Vendor Shop Surveillance Auditing: Training third-party inspectors to execute structured factory audits and non-conformance logging.
  • Phase-Gate Quality Control: Implementing mechanical completion and pre-commissioning check-sheets to reduce project handover liabilities.
07

Quantitative Operational Excellence (Lean Six Sigma for Heavy Industry)

Statistical process control tailored specifically for manufacturing yards, fabrication shops, and large-scale industrial projects.

  • DMAIC Methodology Deployment: Root-cause analysis frameworks applied directly to reducing welding defect rates and structural non-conformances.
  • Statistical Process Control (SPC): Utilizing control charts and capability indices (Cp/Cpk) to stabilize fabrication and casting yard production lines.

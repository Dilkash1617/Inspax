Independent Third-Party Inspection & Asset Integrity Management

Inspax Inspection and Certification Consultants is a trusted provider of engineering advisory, specialized technical training, and multi-disciplinary inspection regimes. We partner with asset owners, EPC contractors, and manufacturers to mitigate operational risk, maintain regulatory compliance, and verify structural and material integrity across the project lifecycle.

Our multidisciplinary team provides precise, evidence-based assessments for the manufacturing, construction, energy, and logistics sectors, from advanced Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) and structural safety audits to BGAS and NACE-compliant coating and corrosion inspections.

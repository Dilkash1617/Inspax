Our Expertise

Independent Third-Party Inspection & Technical Audits

Inspax provides independent inspection and quality surveillance services throughout the manufacturing, fabrication, construction and industrial supply chain. Our technical approach focuses on verifying compliance with applicable engineering specifications, international standards and project quality requirements. From mechanical equipment and static assets to protective coatings and heavy infrastructure, our inspection services help identify non-conformities, strengthen quality assurance and support reliable project execution.

Mechanical Inspection

Mechanical & Static Equipment Quality Surveillance

We deliver rigorous, objective technical surveillance throughout the fabrication, assembly and deployment phases of critical static assets.

  • Line Pipes & Cross-Country Pipeline Components Mill inspections, longitudinal weld profiling, dimensional verification and geometric compliance.
  • Storage Tanks & Pressure Vessels Structural integrity verification against applicable ASME Section VIII and API requirements during fabrication.
  • Skid Packages & Modular Assemblies Structural alignment, bolting verification and piping manifold validation.
  • Process Equipment Assurance Technical inspection of heavy columns, boilers, refinery units and other process equipment prior to site delivery.
Coating & Corrosion Inspection

Protective Coatings & NACE / BGAS Corrosion Audits

Independent field and factory evaluations designed to prevent premature coating failures, monitor application quality and reduce environmental degradation.

  • Surface Preparation Audits Quantitative assessment of substrate cleanliness, surface profile, anchor patterns and soluble salt contamination before coating application.
  • Coating Application Surveillance Monitoring of Dry Film Thickness (DFT), Wet Film Thickness (WFT), environmental conditions and inter-coat curing requirements.
  • Advanced Holiday Detection High-voltage and low-voltage spark testing to identify pinholes, voids and discontinuities in protective coatings and linings.
  • Asset Degradation Mapping Systematic assessment of existing infrastructure to classify corrosion severity and recommend appropriate technical rehabilitation pathways.
Civil & Structural Inspection

Heavy Infrastructure & Structural Supply Chain QA/QC

Independent engineering validation across the civil and structural supply chain to identify substandard material fabrication and ensure compliance before materials and components reach the construction site.

01 / STRUCTURAL STEEL

Structural Steel Fabrication Audits

Technical oversight at casting yards and fabrication shops, including monitoring of Welding Procedure Specifications (WPS), welding activities and structural dimensional verification.

02 / CONCRETE ELEMENTS

Precast & Prestressed Concrete Element Audits

Dimensional control, tendon tensioning surveillance and curing log verification for high-load infrastructure components.

03 / MATERIAL VERIFICATION

Vendor Material Traceability Audits

Technical verification and traceability of Material Test Reports (MTRs), mill certificates and heat numbers to identify potential material non-compliance.

04 / QUALITY ASSURANCE

Supply Chain Quality Surveillance

Independent quality monitoring of fabrication, material procurement and manufacturing activities to support project specifications and quality plans.

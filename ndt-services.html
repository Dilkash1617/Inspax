Our Expertise

Industrial Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) Services

Our Non-Destructive Testing methodologies provide advanced surface and volumetric inspection techniques to identify subsurface flaws, material discontinuities and weld defects without causing damage to industrial components or disrupting asset integrity.

Standard Inspection

Conventional NDT Services

  • Visual Testing (VT) & Remote Visual Inspection (RVI) High-resolution drone technology and advanced optical instruments for detailed asset monitoring and inspection.
  • Surface & Flaw Detection Dye Penetrant Testing (DPT) and Magnetic Particle Inspection (MPI) to identify surface-breaking defects.
  • Ultrasonic Inspections Ultrasonic Flaw Detection and Ultrasonic Thickness Gauging (UTG) to evaluate internal defects, wall thinning and corrosion.
  • Radiography Testing (RT) Conventional and Digital Radiography Examination for internal volumetric inspection of welds and materials.
Advanced Inspection Technology

Advanced NDT Services

We provide advanced inspection technologies designed for critical industrial assets, high-integrity welds and comprehensive pipeline condition assessment.

01 / ADVANCED ULTRASONICS

Phased Array Ultrasonic Testing (PAUT) & TOFD

High-precision weld scanning using Phased Array Ultrasonic Testing and Time of Flight Diffraction for advanced defect detection, characterization and sizing.

02 / ELECTROMAGNETIC

Electromagnetic Testing

Specialized Eddy Current Testing (ECT) and Pulse Eddy Current Testing (PECT) to detect material discontinuities and assess corrosion through protective coatings.

03 / PIPELINE INSPECTION

Pipeline Integrity Management

Magnetic Flux Leakage (MFL), Long Range Ultrasonic Testing (LRUT) and Electronic Geometry Pigging for comprehensive pipeline condition assessment.

04 / WELD INSPECTION

Advanced Weld Inspection

Advanced inspection techniques for identifying, evaluating and characterizing critical weld defects and material discontinuities.

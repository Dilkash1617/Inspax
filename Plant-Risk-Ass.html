Our Expertise

Industrial Plant Risk Assessment & Structural Integrity Evaluation

Industrial facilities operate with complex structural systems, heavy equipment and interconnected process infrastructure. Identifying potential vulnerabilities before they develop into serious operational issues is essential for maintaining safe and reliable operations. Inspax provides engineering-focused plant risk assessments and structural audits to evaluate the physical condition of critical assets, identify structural deficiencies and support informed maintenance and rehabilitation decisions. Our assessment methodology focuses on structural performance, foundation integrity, mechanical stresses and industrial safety barriers.

Structural Engineering

Structural Stability & Foundations Integrity Audits

Rigorous engineering examinations of primary load-bearing frameworks to identify structural degradation and evaluate the condition and performance of critical foundations.

  • Load-Bearing Infrastructure Diagnostics Structural integrity mapping of heavy industrial steel frames, crane gantry girders and reinforced concrete columns.
  • Concrete Degradation & Corrosion Mapping Assessment of carbonation depth, chloride ingress and reinforcement corrosion across high-exposure industrial foundations.
  • Structural Fatigue & Settlement Analysis Monitoring and assessment of differential foundation settlement, structural deflection and dynamic vibration-related fatigue under operational loads.
  • Seismic & Wind Load Compliance Evaluation of existing structural elements against applicable structural engineering codes to identify potential strengthening and reinforcement requirements.
Industrial Risk Assessment

Industrial Plant Risk Profiling & Safety Barrier Mapping

Objective, engineering-driven technical risk evaluations designed to identify structural hazards, assess operational vulnerabilities and evaluate the effectiveness of physical protection systems.

  • Physical Safety Barrier Mapping Spatial analysis and evaluation of structural containment zones, blast walls, high-pressure relief barriers and hazard isolation zones.
  • Asset Risk Profiling Consequence-driven technical assessment of critical structural components to classify failure risks and support inspection prioritization.
  • Operational Boundary Audits Engineering calculations and verification of industrial plant configurations against applicable original design limits and operating envelopes.
Mechanical & Structural Integrity

Mechanical Stress Integrity & Pipe Rack Audits

Structural assessment of interconnected process piping networks, overhead support frames and secondary structural elements supporting high-temperature and high-pressure industrial operations.

  • Pipe Rack Structural Evaluations Weight-load reassessment, structural connection reviews and corrosion monitoring of critical overhead process pipe racks.
  • Dynamic Equipment Support Audits Frequency and stress assessment of foundations supporting heavy rotating machinery, pumps and compressors.
  • Thermal Stress & Anchor Point Mapping Evaluation of structural restraints, guides, supports and expansion joints to help prevent excessive stress transfer to supporting structures.
